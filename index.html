Authentic Pakistani flavors, hearty slow cooks, fragrant rice and charcoal-grilled favorites — prepared to order with halal ingredients.

From biryani and pulao to karahi, naan, rolls and charcoal BBQ, our menu brings together the classic flavors of Pakistani cooking.

Our menu is built around food that takes time: slow-cooked curries, aromatic rice, tandoor breads and grilled dishes finished over charcoal.

Whether you're stopping in for a plate of biryani or sharing a mixed grill, Biryani House keeps the focus on bold flavor and made-to-order food.
